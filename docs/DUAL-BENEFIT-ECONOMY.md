# Pretty Explosion — Dual-Benefit Economy

**North star:** A **BC Canada AI revenue-generating company** — beautiful for humans, agents, and AI — where every product path is **financially useful to clients** and **accumulates capital for Pretty Explosion (PE)**.

**Company shape:** Not locked to film. **Quality Video / media is a beachhead**; the machine opens to SaaS, automation, B2B tools, and other cash-compounding AI products.

**Themes that matter:** payment links · energy (prepaid usage) · purchasing power · creative expression · promotion / distribution.

**Jurisdiction:** British Columbia, Canada. This document is **educational / strategic only** — **not** legal, tax, securities, or investment advice. Before go-live, fundraising, marketplace design, or anything that looks like investing/lending/custody, consult a **BC-qualified lawyer and accountant**, and **Canadian securities counsel** (BCSC / CSA) where capital or platform fees are involved.

**Currency lens:** Plan in **CAD** unless a contract says otherwise. Numbers below are **illustrative sketches**, not forecasts, traction, or AUM.

**What PE is not:** Not a bank, broker-dealer, investment fund, or licensed money-services business unless and until properly registered. Do not invent licensed products.

---

## 1. Ethos — mutual benefit

Pretty Explosion wins only when clients win in **cash, capacity, or reach** — and PE keeps a clear, disclosed slice of that value as **margin, recurring revenue, or platform take**.

| Principle | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Client first cash** | Products should help clients **get paid, ship more, spend less friction, or reach buyers** — not just “use AI.” |
| **PE capital second (but real)** | Every module has a **revenue line** PE can book: fee, subscription, usage, take-rate, or success fee. |
| **Transparent exchange** | Clients know what they pay and what they get. No hidden “yield,” no fake returns. |
| **Human approve** | AI accelerates; **humans approve** client-facing craft and money moves where quality or liability matters. |
| **Beautiful for all three** | Interfaces and workflows should feel good for **people**, be legible to **agents**, and be efficient for **AI systems** (clear APIs, credits, status). |
| **BC-honest** | Canadian/BC compliance posture first; expand markets only with counsel. |

**One-liner:** *If it doesn’t help the client make or save money (or ship more of what they sell), it doesn’t ship — and if it doesn’t leave margin or recurring cash for PE, it isn’t a product, it’s a hobby.*

## 2. Core loop — Client value ↔ PE capital

```
  Client need (get paid / create / promote / fund)
           │
           ▼
  PE module (tools + AI speed + human approve)
           │
     ┌─────┴─────┐
     ▼           ▼
 Client outcome   PE capture
 (cash, credits,  (fee, sub,
  reach, time)     usage, take)
           │           │
           └─────┬─────┘
                 ▼
        Reinvest in better modules
        (more client value → more PE capital)
```

**Flywheel tests (pass both):**

1. **Client test:** After using PE, can they point to *more money in*, *more shipped*, or *less waste*?
2. **PE test:** Can PE point to *booked CAD* that is not contingent fantasy — fee, MRR, usage, or closed success fee?

If only one side passes, redesign. Mutual benefit is the brand; **capital accumulation** is how the AI revenue machine compounds.

**Beachhead entry:** Media / Quality Video is the fastest path to real client outcomes (promo that sells) and real PE invoices. Broader modules (payments UX, credits, promotion OS) attach to that trust — then expand beyond film.

## 3. Product modules

Each module lists **user benefit**, **PE revenue**, and a **BC caution** where lawyers/accountants (or securities counsel) are needed. Nothing here is a licensed product claim.

### 3.1 Payment links / checkout (Stripe etc. in Canada)

| Side | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **User benefit** | Clients send a **payment link** or hosted checkout and get paid in CAD (or multi-currency if enabled) without building their own storefront. Fit for deposits on video packages, memberships, merch, tickets, retainers. |
| **PE revenue** | (a) **Implementation / setup fee** for branded checkout flows; (b) **monthly OS fee** for portal + link management; (c) optional **success / facilitation fee** on PE-originated invoices (disclosed); (d) PE’s **own** Stripe (or equivalent) for studio packages and memberships — PE keeps 100% of its product price after processor fees. |
| **BC caution** | Using **Stripe (or similar) as a processor** for PE’s own sales is ordinary commerce. **Collecting, holding, or transmitting funds for others**, offering wallets, or anything that looks like money transmission / MSB may trigger **FINTRAC / provincial** obligations — **lawyer before** multi-party payouts or “PE holds your money.” Tax: GST/HST and BC PST with an **accountant**. PE is **not** a bank. |

**Design note:** Prefer **direct-to-client Stripe Connect / standard checkout** patterns where the client’s money goes to the **client’s** merchant account; PE bills for software/service separately. That keeps the dual benefit clean and reduces “we’re holding funds” risk.

---

### 3.2 Purchasing power (credits, bundles, membership)

| Side | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **User benefit** | **Credits, bundles, and memberships** that increase what clients can ship: more previz rounds, more cutdowns, more grant-match runs, more AI assists per month — clear “I can do more” purchasing power, not vague points. |
| **PE revenue** | **Prepaid credit packs** (high contribution margin once COGS known); **monthly/annual membership** (MRR); **bundle upsells** attached to Spark / Pulse / Explosion; unused credits expire on disclosed terms (fair, written). |
| **BC caution** | Prepaid credits are generally **gift-card / consumer-contract** territory if sold to consumers — disclosure, expiry rules, and refund policy need a **BC lawyer**. Do **not** market credits as an “investment” or appreciating asset. Accountant for deferred revenue treatment. |

**Purchasing-power promise (plain language):** *Buy capacity to create and ship — not a stake in PE.*

---

### 3.3 Energy (usage credits / compute / production energy)

| Side | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **User benefit** | “Energy” made concrete: **prepaid usage credits** for AI compute, render/previz minutes, assistant runs, or production throughput. Clients see a clear meter: energy in → work out. |
| **PE revenue** | Sell **energy packs** and **auto-refill**; margin = price − model/API/render COGS − support. Tiered rates (member vs pay-as-you-go). |
| **BC caution** | Same as credits: **not securities**, not yield. Disclose burn rates and that unused energy may expire. If energy is only a metaphor for PE’s internal capacity, still price and invoice as **services/prepaid services** with counsel on consumer terms. |

**Metaphor → product:** Energy = **metered prepaid service units**, CAD-denominated, redeemable only for PE products — never “earn interest.”

---

### 3.4 Creative expression tools (AI-assisted create, human approve)

| Side | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **User benefit** | AI-assisted creation (scripts, boards, cuts, grant copy drafts, promo variants) with a hard rule: **human approve** before client-facing or paid publish. Faster **brief → preview → approve**; more creative expression per dollar and hour. |
| **PE revenue** | Included in **packages** (beachhead video); **seat / workspace SaaS**; **per-generation or energy** drawdowns; premium **director-in-the-loop** hours at service rates. |
| **BC caution** | Copyright / likeness / training-data and **AI disclosure** to clients — lawyer for templates and SOWs. Employment/contractor classification if freelancers use the tools. Privacy: **PIPEDA / BC PIPA** for personal information in prompts and assets. |

---

### 3.5 Promotion / distribution amplification

| Side | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **User benefit** | Amplify reach of finished work: cutdowns, platform packages, distribution checklists, paid boost **coordination**, partner/playlist intros where PE has relationships — so creative expression **gets seen and converts**. |
| **PE revenue** | **Promo retainers**; **campaign add-ons** to Explosion-tier work; **performance-adjacent fees** only when measurable and contracted (careful: not fake ROAS claims); later **marketplace listing** fees if a catalog exists. |
| **BC caution** | Advertising standards and influencer/disclosure rules; no guaranteed outcomes. Paid media spend should preferably flow **client → ad platform**; PE invoices for strategy/creative/management. Lottery/contest mechanics need separate legal review. |

---

### 3.6 Optional — Grants / funding OS (capital *access* for clients)

| Side | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **User benefit** | Help clients **find and apply** for grants and funding programs (Grant Intelligence path): match, drafts, submission hygiene — **access to capital opportunities**, not PE lending money. |
| **PE revenue** | **Membership**; **success fees** on awarded amounts (contingent — don’t fund payroll on them alone); optional **done-with-you** package fees. |
| **BC caution** | PE is **not** offering securities, syndicating deals, or acting as a broker unless registered. Success-fee contracts: **lawyer**. Grant program rules vary — no invented win rates. Tax treatment of fees: **accountant**. If ever facilitating investor intros, that is a **different regulated path** — stop and get securities counsel first. |

## 4. Dual-sided unit economics (illustrative sketch)

**No fake traction.** Fill real CAD when Luke has costs and prices. Formulas only:

### Client side (value)

| Lever | Sketch |
|-------|--------|
| Time saved | Hours of previz / copy / admin × client’s opportunity cost |
| Cash accelerated | Deposits collected via payment links sooner; fewer unpaid starts |
| Capacity gained | Extra shippable assets per credit/membership period |
| Reach | Incremental qualified views / leads from promo packs (measure; don’t invent) |

### PE side (capture)

| Lever | Sketch |
|-------|--------|
| Contribution margin | Price − real COGS (API, freelancers, ads spend if PE pays, support) |
| Recurring | Membership MRR − churn − support load |
| Usage | Energy/credit burn × margin per unit |
| Take-rate | Only if PE runs a true marketplace — see §5; start near **zero** marketplace complexity |
| Success fees | Awarded × contracted % — **contingent**; ring-fence |

### Dual check (per module)

```
Client surplus  ≈  outcome value − what they paid PE
PE surplus      ≈  what they paid PE − PE COGS − attributable overhead
```

Both should be **positive in expectation** after learning cycles. If client surplus is negative, churn and brand damage; if PE surplus is negative, it’s a charity feature — rename it marketing spend or cut it.

**Package beachhead (qualitative):** Spark / Pulse / Explosion priced after real COGS + contingency (often discussed ~10–20%); track contribution **per job**. Attach credits/energy as **upsell**, not as a loss-leader that never converts.

## 5. Capital accumulation paths for PE

How PE **stores and compounds** capital without pretending to be a fund:

| Path | How it accumulates | Notes |
|------|--------------------|-------|
| **Gross margin on services** | Video / creative / promo jobs | Beachhead cash; fund buffer (1–2 delivery cycles). |
| **Recurring membership** | Grants OS, creative seats, energy auto-refill | Stabilizes CAD runway; watch churn. |
| **Prepaid credits / energy** | Deferred revenue → recognized on burn | Improves cash timing; accountant for recognition. |
| **Software / OS fees** | Studio portal, payment-link OS, B2B white-label | Higher margin thesis; support load is the tax. |
| **Disclosed success fees** | Grants (and later carefully scoped outcomes) | Upside only; never baseline payroll. |
| **Marketplace take-rate** | Optional later | **High regulatory care** — platform liability, tax, possible securities if “investable” listings appear. Prefer **software fee + optional featured placement** before classic take-rate. Counsel before launch. |

**Treasury habit (from Mini Colosus):** ring-fence delivery float vs founder draw vs contingent receivables; weekly cash view; monthly runway. See [`trading-finance-beside-product.md`](./trading-finance-beside-product.md).

**Reinvestment order (suggested):** (1) delivery buffer, (2) AI/toolchain that raises margin or speed, (3) GTM for recurring modules, (4) optional new AI product lines — not speculative trading infra.

## 6. What NOT to do

| Anti-pattern | Why it’s out |
|--------------|--------------|
| **Ponzi vibes** | Paying old clients from new clients’ money dressed as “returns.” |
| **Unregistered securities** | Selling “shares of revenue,” tokenized yield, investable credit appreciation, or pooled returns without Canadian securities compliance. |
| **Fake yield** | “Your energy earns 8%/month” or any return language on prepaid balances. |
| **Invented traction** | Fake ARR, AUM, ROAS, grant win rates, or $ COGS on site/investor pages. |
| **Pretending to be a bank / broker** | Custody of client funds, investment advice, deal brokerage — unless properly licensed. |
| **Guaranteed outcomes** | Guaranteed grant awards, viral reach, or investment returns. |
| **Contingent-only payroll** | Staffing against unpaid success fees. |
| **Dark pattern credits** | Buried expiry, impossible redemption, bait pricing. |
| **US-only compliance copy** | PE is **BC / Canada first**; don’t paste SEC-only disclaimers as if they were enough. |

**Hard line:** Prepaid credits and energy are **product fuel**, not financial products.

## 7. 90-day beachhead — fastest mutual cash in BC

**Goal:** Real CAD to clients (or clear capacity) **and** real CAD to PE within 90 days, with minimal regulatory surface.

### Days 0–30 — Sell the beachhead, instrument payments

1. **Close 1–3 Quality Video jobs** (Spark/Pulse) with written SOWs — human approve on delivery.  
2. **PE checkout live** for deposits/final invoices via Stripe (or Canadian-capable processor) on **PE’s** merchant account — PE revenue = package price − processor fees − COGS.  
3. **Optional for clients:** teach/configure **their** payment links for their own customers (setup fee) — money does **not** sit with PE.  
4. Accountant kickoff: GST/HST, BC PST, invoicing templates.

### Days 31–60 — Attach purchasing power + energy

5. Launch a **simple credit/energy pack** redeemable for previz rounds, cutdowns, or Grant AI assists — disclosed expiry/refund rules via lawyer review.  
6. Offer a **thin membership** (e.g. monthly grant match + N energy units) for recurring CAD.  
7. Track contribution per job and pack **with real numbers** (no invented COGS).

### Days 61–90 — Creative + promo loop; decide what scales

8. Productize **brief → preview → approve** in a lightweight portal (even Notion + Stripe is fine at first).  
9. Sell **one promo amplification add-on** (cutdown + distribution checklist / boost management) on a closed job — case study with permission.  
10. **Go / no-go:** double down on video+credits+membership **or** add Grant success-fee contracts (lawyer) — **not** marketplace take-rate yet.

### Explicitly out of the first 90 days

- Marketplace with investable listings  
- Holding client funds  
- Yield language  
- Broad public fundraising without securities counsel  
- Building “bank-like” wallets  

### Success signals (mutual)

| Client | PE |
|--------|----|
| Paid invoice or prepaid credits they actually burn to ship | Positive contribution on ≥1 package type |
| Faster approve cycle they notice | Recurring membership ≥1 paying (even small) |
| Promo asset in market | Buffer toward 1 delivery cycle of costs |
