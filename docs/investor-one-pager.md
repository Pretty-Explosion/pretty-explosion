# Pretty Explosion — Investor One-Pager

**Tagline / wedge:** Cinema-grade promo, AI-accelerated, human-directed.

Pretty Explosion is a future-forward film & media company. The flagship is **Quality Video Promotion** — premium commercial and promo production that sells today — with two flanking engines that compound distribution, trust, and creator demand.

---

## North star

BC Canada **AI revenue company**. Quality Video is the **beachhead**; grants, Studio OS, and broader AI SaaS/automation/B2B tools compound cash. Not film-production-only forever. Educational only — no invented traction or AUM.

Phases: **A** Video cash engine → **B** Grant membership/fees → **C** Studio OS / AI product surface → **D** B2B + new AI lines. Detail: `docs/AI-REVENUE-MACHINE.md`.

## Unit economics / margin play

Qualitative margin levers (not projections):

- **Shot libraries** — reusable cinematic plates and motifs across briefs
- **AI previz** — boards, animatics, and rough assembly that compress alignment time
- **Templated finish** — color, captions, aspect variants, and delivery kits without reinventing the pipeline each job

Directors keep taste, framing, and performance; AI accelerates the repetitive path from brief to masters.
