## 1. Role of finance next to the product

Pretty Explosion is a **BC Canada AI revenue company**. **Quality Video** (Spark / Pulse / Explosion) and **Grant Intelligence** are early engines / beachheads — not a permanent ceiling. Broader **AI SaaS, automation, and B2B tools** can join as they prove contribution margin.

Finance sits beside products as **operating treasury + unit economics discipline**, not as a separate “trading desk” product or AUM business.

**Goal:** keep CAD cash available for delivery cycles, price so margin survives delays, and make Seed uses readable to partners.

**North star fit:** finance supports the **AI revenue machine** — compounding cash from AI-accelerated products.

---

## 2. Treasury for production / delivery cashflow (CAD / Canadian banking norms)

- **Operating buffer:** hold roughly **1–2 delivery cycles** of cost coverage so a late client payment does not stall the next job (video package **or** software delivery/support batch).
- **Ring-fence:** separate **delivery float** from founder draw and from contingent success-fee receivables still outstanding.
- **Simple cadence:** weekly cash view (in / committed / out); monthly runway vs booked pipeline (+ recurring when live).
- **Banking norms (high level):** ordinary Canadian business operating accounts in CAD; track sales tax (GST/HST and BC PST where applicable) with an accountant; avoid mixing personal and operating funds.
- **No exotic instruments needed at Phase 0** — clarity of burn and receivables beats yield-seeking.
- **No invented $ COGS** — fill real costs when Luke provides them.

## 3. Illustrative revenue & unit economics *(examples only)*

| Layer | What it is | Finance lens |
|-------|------------|--------------|
| Spark / Pulse / Explosion | Beachhead video packages | Price after real COGS + contingency (often ~10–20%); track **contribution margin** per package |
| Agency white-label | Volume via partners | Lower margin, higher utilization — watch capacity |
| Grant / other memberships | Recurring | Stabilize cash; LTV vs acquisition once live |
| Grant success fees | Contingent upside | Don’t count until awarded; buffer rebuild, not baseline payroll |
| AI SaaS / automation / B2B | Software & tools | High gross-margin thesis; watch CAC, churn, support |

**Unit check (placeholders only):** contribution ≈ price − real COGS. Seed should fund enough cycles to prove margin at real delivery speed. **Do not invent dollar COGS.**

## 5. Risk notes *(label clearly)*

- Client concentration and payment delay → treasury buffer  
- Overruns eating margin → contingency + change-order / scope rules  
- Success-fee timing → never fund fixed costs on contingent revenue alone  
- Scope creep → written SOWs / product specs  
- Expanding beyond media → still no fake ARR; validate willingness to pay  
- Regulatory / fundraising → Canadian securities + BC privacy/tax professionals before solicitations or public claims
