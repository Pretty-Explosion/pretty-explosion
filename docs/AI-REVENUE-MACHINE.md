# Pretty Explosion — AI Revenue Machine

**North star:** Become a **BC Canada AI revenue-generating company** — a revenue-growing machine.  
**Success:** AI-accelerated products that **compound cash** for clients and for Pretty Explosion (PE).

**Company shape:** Not locked to film production. **Quality Video / media is a beachhead vertical** (proven craft + AI speed). The roadmap deliberately opens to **broader AI products** — SaaS, automation, B2B tools, and other cash-compounding software — as capacity and demand allow.

**Jurisdiction:** Based in **British Columbia, Canada**. This roadmap is **educational / strategic only** — not legal, tax, securities, or investment advice. For decisions, consult a **BC-qualified lawyer and accountant** (and Canadian securities counsel for any capital path; BCSC / CSA frameworks).

**Currency lens:** Plan treasury in **CAD** unless a contract says otherwise. Site package ranges remain illustrative placeholders, not forecasts. **No invented traction, AUM, or $ COGS.**

## Mini Colosus — treasury & unit econ (high level)

Finance sits **beside** products as **operating treasury + unit-economics discipline** (CAD / Canadian banking norms).

### Treasury

- **Operating buffer:** ~**1–2 delivery cycles** of cost coverage so late receivables don’t stall the next job (video shoot/edit **or** SaaS onboarding/support batch — same idea).
- **Ring-fence:** production / delivery float vs founder draw vs contingent success-fee receivables.
- **Cadence:** weekly cash (in / committed / out); monthly runway vs pipeline + recurring MRR when live.
- **Banking:** ordinary Canadian CAD business accounts; GST/HST and BC PST awareness with an accountant. Phase 0 needs clarity, not exotic instruments.
- **No invented $ COGS** — fill when Luke provides real costs.

### Contribution margin (qualitative)

| Layer | Finance lens |
|-------|----------------|
| Spark / Pulse / Explosion (beachhead) | Price after real COGS + contingency (~10–20% often discussed); track contribution per package |
| Grant / other memberships | Recurring stabilizer; LTV vs acquisition once live |
| Success fees | Contingent — buffer rebuild, not baseline payroll |
| SaaS / automation / B2B tools | High gross-margin thesis; watch CAC, churn, support load |
| White-label | Lower margin, higher utilization — watch capacity |

### Seed uses (~CAD $150–400k **band** — uses story, not a raise ask)

Typical buckets (percentages Luke can defend):

- Delivery float / toolchain (video **and** early AI product infra)  
- People / ops matched to mix  
- Grant Intelligence + next AI product GTM  
- Working capital (deposits, insurance, compliance basics)

Any raise: **Canadian securities counsel (BC focus)**. See `/invest` disclaimer.
