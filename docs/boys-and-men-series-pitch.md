## Logline
*Boys and Men* is a character-driven series about how boys become men — and how men keep becoming — told with high-skill cinematography, honest intimacy, and room for public partners to help shape the story.

## Tone & craft
- Documentary-adjacent drama energy: lived-in frames, golden-hour and low-key night, no stock gloss
- Inclusive casting and story consult; AI assists speed (assemblies, variants) — humans keep the director’s chair
- Dual-benefit: audiences get clarity and care; partners get a public project that builds capacity, not extractive spectacle

## Who it’s for
- Viewers who want coming-of-age / manhood stories without cynicism or doom-scroll framing
- Studios and streamers looking for series IP with poster-ready visual identity
- Grant funders and public institutions that back arts, youth, mental health, community storytelling
- Public partners (nonprofits, schools, community orgs) who want to champion it as a shared public project

## Three doors (same spine, different ask)

### 1. Studios / streamers
**Ask:** Development or series partnership  
**What they get:** Series bible + lookbook energy (poster already exists), craft pipeline via Pretty Explosion Studio (brief → preview → approve), BC-based production partner  
**What we need back:** Feedback, option/development path, or co-pro conversation (Luke-led; no outreach until directed)

### 2. Grants
**Ask:** Development / production support for a public-interest series  
**What they get:** Clear themes (identity, care, community), transparent process, inclusive storytelling  
**What we need back:** Fit with funder priorities; Grant Intelligence match → outline → approve with Luke

### 3. Public partners
**Ask:** Champion *Boys and Men* as a public project — amplify, co-host, or help shape community touchpoints  
**What they get:** Credit-forward collaboration, preview rights on outreach cuts, dual-benefit framing (not extractive “awareness” theater)  
**What we need back:** Interest letter or soft commit to explore; no fake traction claims
