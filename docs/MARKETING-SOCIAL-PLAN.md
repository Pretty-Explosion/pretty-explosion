# Marketing & social — 30-day plan (Pretty Explosion)

**Goal:** Grow awareness of PE as a **BC Canada AI revenue company** with a **dual-benefit** story: clients get smoother growth (video, grants, later AI tools); PE compounds cash ethically.
**Beachhead:** Quality Video Promotion. **Not locked to film** — content can preview broader AI products without abandoning craft.
**Jurisdiction tone:** Educational; no fake licenses, no securities pitch on social, no crypto-as-investment hype.
**Honesty lock:** **No fake follower counts, engagement rates, case-study metrics, or "as seen in" claims** until Luke supplies real ones.

**Channels (primary):** LinkedIn · Instagram · X (Twitter) · short video (Reels / Shorts / native vertical).
**Cadence target:** ~5–6 posts/week across channels (repurpose aggressively). Quality over volume.

---

## Positioning pillars (reuse in every week)

1. **Cinema-grade promo, AI speed, human craft** — brief → preview → approve
2. **Grant Intelligence** — fairer funding access; AI drafts, humans own submissions
3. **Dual-benefit** — what helps the client also builds PE's AI revenue machine
4. **Truth-seeking journalism** (lighter cadence) — solutions + accountability, not doom
5. **BC / Canada roots** — serious ops, CAD-first payments; crypto only as future optional rails (see CRYPTO-PAYMENTS-BC.md) — never the hero of growth marketing

---

## Voice & creative locks

- Brand: **galactic** energy in covers/thumbnails; **blackhole** mark in profiles / end cards when assets exist
- Tone: confident, calm, inclusive; avoid hype-bro crypto or "guaranteed ROI"
- Disclose AI when showing AI-assisted process; credit human direction on client-facing craft
- CTAs: /start, studio packages, Grant AI — not "invest now" as a social CTA
